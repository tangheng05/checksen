What you can send

  • A message

    Checks for tricks scammers use in Khmer and English: asking for an OTP, fees before a loan, job or prize, threats to close your account, and fake police.

  • A link

    Shows where it really leads, whether the site is brand new, and whether it imitates a bank, wallet, Telegram or government site.

  • A KHQR code

    Shows the real recipient name, account, bank and amount, and checks whether the code was edited.

  • A screenshot

    Reads the text in an SMS or chat screenshot and checks it like a message.

  • A file

    Warns when a file is really an app in disguise, such as .apk or .pdf.scr.

How to read a reply

  • 🔴 High risk

    Clear scam signs. Don't pay, and don't share any code.

  • 🟠 Suspicious

    Some warning signs. Check through an official number or app before doing anything.

  • ⚪ No scam signs found yet

    No known tricks were found, but CheckSen never says a message is safe.

  • ❔ Can't tell

    The image was unclear or couldn't be read. Send it again.

Every reply reminds you to check the recipient's name in your own bank app before paying.

Common scam signs in Cambodia

Banks, ministries and police in Cambodia warn about these tricks again and again.

  • Asks for an OTP or PIN, even when it claims to be your bank.

  • Asks for a fee, tax or deposit before you get a loan, job or prize.

  • Threatens to freeze or close your account unless you click a link now.

  • Claims to be a relative with a new number who needs money.

  • Offers high-paid typing or call-centre work in Poipet, Sihanoukville or Bavet.

  • Asks you to pay a power bill or government fee through an agent or a personal account.

Questions people ask

How can I tell if a KHQR code is real or edited?

Send a photo of the QR to CheckSen. It shows the real recipient name, account, bank and amount, and checks the code's check digits. Before paying, always check the recipient's name in your own bank app.

Do banks ever ask for my OTP code by phone or Telegram?

No. Real banks and companies never ask for your OTP code, PIN or password. Anyone who asks for them is most likely a scammer.

Is CheckSen free? Do I need to install an app?

It's free and there's nothing to install. Open @CheckSen_bot in Telegram and send it a message, link or photo.

I already paid a scammer. What should I do?

Call your bank right away on the number on your card or in its app, and ask them to stop the transfer. Keep screenshots of the messages and the payment receipt, and report it to the police. Don't pay anyone who promises to get your money back for a fee.

Can CheckSen tell me a message is safe?

No. It can only say whether it found known scam signs. A new trick may not be caught yet, so when in doubt, check through your bank's official number or app.

Privacy

Your messages are not kept.

CheckSen checks messages and images in memory and doesn't save them. Its code is open, so anyone can check this on GitHub.

Messages and images
Not saved. The reply is kept in memory for an hour, keyed only by a hash of the content.
AI checks
Text and screenshots are sent to Google Gemini's free tier. Google may use them to improve its products and people may review them.Hide account numbers and personal details before you send.
Reports
If you tap ❌ on a KHQR check, a report is stored against that account, linked only to a keyed hash of your Telegram ID. An account shows as reported only after 3 different people report it.
Deleting reports
Send /forget to CheckSen to delete every report linked to you.
