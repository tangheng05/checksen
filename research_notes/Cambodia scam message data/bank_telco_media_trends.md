# Cambodian scam message texts from banks, telcos, media and NGOs, plus 2025–2026 scam trends

Research date: 2026-09-28. Access notes: khmertimeskh.com, phnompenhpost.com, ababank.com and thestar.com.my all return Cloudflare 403 to fetchers. ABA and some Phnom Penh Post pages were read through Wayback Machine snapshots. Khmer Times is excluded from Wayback, so Khmer Times items below come from search-engine summaries only and are marked as such. Cambodianess, CamboJA, Wing Bank, TRC, ABC, VnExpress, Fact Crescendo (via Wayback) and Khmer-language outlets were read directly.

## Q1. What example scam messages have Cambodian banks, e-wallets and the NBC published?

### Takeaway
Only Wing Bank publishes short example scam messages. ABA publishes descriptions and lookalike domains but no sample message text. ACLEDA's warning (a 24-hour "verify your account or it will be frozen" SMS) is known only through Fact Crescendo's paraphrase. I found no public sample texts from Canadia, Prince, Sathapana, AMK, PRASAC, Hattha, Chip Mong, TrueMoney, Pi Pay or Bakong/NBC.

### Cited Findings
- Wing Bank's Scam Awareness page lists four example scam messages: "You've won a cash prize from Wing Bank. Pay 50,000 Riel to claim!", "Instant loan approved! Just send your ID and pay the small service fee.", "We can help you withdraw your Wing account money faster — share your OTP.", "Click this link to verify your Wing account security." — [Wing Bank Scam Awareness](https://www.wingbank.com.kh/en/company/security-awareness-center/scam-awareness)
- On the same page, Wing lists these red flags:
  - Lookalike domains, e.g. "wingbank-online.com, wingapp.xyz".
  - Telegram groups with names that look like Wing, e.g. "Wing Loan Khmer".
  - Pages and groups that ask for ID photos, OTP, a 4-digit PIN or a "commission fee".
  - Fake apps whose publisher is not "Wing Bank (Cambodia) Plc."
  - Fake staff who ask the customer to install remote-access tools.
  - Wing's only official contact channels are its app, its verified Facebook page and hotline 023 999 989.
  - Source: [Wing Bank](https://www.wingbank.com.kh/en/company/security-awareness-center/scam-awareness)
- ABA says scam SMS and emails "can contain links to external resources that may look very similar to the official resources of ABA Bank but have wrong spelling or skip a letter." It also says scammers "alert you that someone got access to your bank account and you need to provide your login credentials to the sender in order to regain the access." — [ABA "Stay protected from scammers!" (Wayback)](https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/stay-protected-from-scammers-8/)
- ABA lists fake sender domains: advancebankasia.xyz, ababankofasla.xyz, advancebankofasia.xyz and ababnkofasla.xyz. It lists fake websites ababkonline.com and ababnkonline.com. ABA says it does not "assign third parties to contact you to claim donation prizes, inheritance, gifts, etc." — [ABA "Be aware of financial scammers!"](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/be-aware-of-financial-scammers/)
- ABA warns about fake Facebook pages that invite people "to join a cash giveaway that requires you to provide access to your bank account". Its genuine page carries a blue checkmark. — [ABA "Stay protected from scammers!" -11](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/stay-protected-from-scammers-11/)
- ABA's QR-code scam notice says scammers "print and affix their own QR codes over legitimate merchant QR codes", which sends payments to the scammer's account. ABA tells payers to check the receiver's name before paying. — [ABA "Protect yourself from QR code scams"](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/protect-yourself-from-qr-code-scams/). The post date is unclear: every archived ABA page shows "7 December 2023", which looks like page chrome.
- ABA warns about Telegram groups promoting crypto trading that falsely name ABA as a payment channel. It cites NBC Notification B7.017.1058, dated 5 Dec 2017, which bars crypto in the banking system. — [ABA "Beware of scammers"](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/beware-of-scammers/)
- ABA says it uses only 023 225 333, 098 203 333 and 098 203 203 for promotions, surveys and account issues, plus toll-free 1 800 203 203. — [ABA](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/stay-protected-from-scammers-8/); [ABA](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/stay-vigilant-to-protect-your-bank-account/)
- ACLEDA and the scam SMS asking customers to verify their account:
  - The SMS asked recipients to click a link "within 24 hours to verify their bank account; otherwise, the account balance of those users would be frozen or blocked."
  - ACLEDA's Facebook page said its staff do not ask for account numbers or personal information by SMS.
  - Source: [Fact Crescendo, published 2024-03-12](https://cambodia.factcrescendo.com/english/a-message-asking-users-to-verify-their-bank-account-is-a-scam/)
- NBC press release of 22 Mar 2023: scammers "use fake social media accounts posing as family members, relatives, friends or institutional leaders", "even going so far as sending fake voice messages and making calls impersonating personal connections, requesting for loans or immediate transfers of money." — [EAC News, 2023-03-23](https://eacnews.asia/home/details/21045)
- In June 2023 an ABA customer lost about $100,000 to "a common SMS 'phishing' attack". The Association of Banks in Cambodia then urged vigilance. — [Cambodia Daily Weekly Dispatch, 9 Jun 2023](https://cambodiadaily.substack.com/p/facebook-probes-hun-sen-100k-lost). A search summary of Open Development Cambodia describes the same loss as a "SIM card-related account hack" ([ODC tag page](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/tag/aba-bank/)). The two sources disagree on the mechanism.

### Inferences
- Only Wing publishes example texts, and they are illustrative and in English, not captured real messages. ABA's material gives lookalike-domain patterns (letter swaps, l for i, dropped "of") rather than message bodies. Those patterns suit a URL-lookalike checker better than message text.
- ACLEDA's case gives a strong template of a 24-hour deadline plus a freeze threat plus a link.

### Gaps
- I could not reach ACLEDA's security tips page (HTTP 503) or ABA's live site (Cloudflare). Wayback held ABA but not ACLEDA.
- I found no published sample messages from Canadia, Prince, Sathapana, AMK, PRASAC, Hattha, Chip Mong, TrueMoney, Pi Pay or Bakong/NBC. These banks mainly post warnings as Facebook images, which search engines do not index.
- I found no NBC or Bakong warning specifically about fake Bakong apps.

## Q2. What have Smart Axiata, Cellcard and Metfone warned about?

### Takeaway
I found no indexed scam warnings from Smart Axiata, Cellcard or Metfone. Search results for "Smart" warnings all belong to Smart Communications in the Philippines, a different company. The telecom-side warnings I found come from regulators: the Telecommunication Regulator of Cambodia (TRC) and the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications (MPTC).

### Cited Findings
- TRC announcement of 2025-09-15 on rising Telegram fraud. It names three tactics:
  - Impersonating public figures and senior officials to promote investment schemes "with promises of high returns in a short period of time".
  - Messages that "falsely inform recipients they have won a prize, then requesting personal or financial information".
  - Posing as Telegram and "sending threatening messages alleging that the recipient's account has been hacked or shows irregular activity, and demanding passwords".
  - Source: [TRC (Wayback)](https://www.trc.gov.kh/en/media/news-releases/7-news-release/); also reported by [Phnom Penh Post](https://phnompenhpost.com/national/trc-warns-of-rise-of-fraudulent-activities-on-telegram-app/)
- MPTC warning on 2022-10-21 about a Telegram takeover lure: a GIF or link saying "Gift 74/100 Free Premium" that asks for name and password. — [Phnom Penh Post, 2022-10-21](https://www.phnompenhpost.com/national/ministry-warns-new-telegram-hack)
- MPTC warning of Aug 2023, quoting a sample message: "Telegram is a computer messaging application focused on security and speed. Your account has not been verified for a long time, please click on the following link within 24 hours, or your account will be deleted". The link then asks for phone number and login code. — [Phnom Penh Post, 2023-08-24](https://www.phnompenhpost.com/national/ministry-issue-scam-warning-telegram-users)
- The Anti-Cyber Crime Department issued a similar Telegram warning on 14 Aug 2023. The lure promised "safe and fast cloud-based computer programmes" and asked users to share phone details. — [Phnom Penh Post, 2023-08-16](https://www.phnompenhpost.com/national/deceptive-links-stay-safe-telegram-users-warned)
- MPTC warning on the "watch a video, get paid" scam (press release 14 Aug 2026):
  - The lure comes through Facebook and TikTok.
  - Victims are asked to join groups, give bank information and install malicious apps.
  - The apps steal OTPs, PINs and passwords.
  - Sources: [Cambodianess, 2026-08-15](https://cambodianess.com/article/public-warned-of-fake-cash-reward-video-scam); [bank-cambodia.org](https://www.bank-cambodia.org/new-scam-alert-watch-a-video-get-paid-offers-are-targeting-cambodian-bank-accounts/)

### Inferences
- A likely telco-related scam type is SIM swap. The 2023 ABA loss hints at it, but I found no telco-published SIM-swap warning.

### Gaps
- There are no Smart, Cellcard or Metfone texts. Their warnings are probably posted as Facebook images or sent as SMS broadcasts, which are not indexed. A manual check of their Facebook pages is needed.

## Q3. What exact scam texts are quoted by English-language Cambodian media, fact-checkers and NGO research?

### Takeaway
The best verbatim sources are Phnom Penh Post (read via Wayback), Fact Crescendo Cambodia (English and Khmer), VnExpress (quoting the Anti-Cyber Crime Department) and ABC (quoting Moses Ngeth's job-ad dataset). Khmer Times covers many relevant stories, but they were only reachable as search summaries.

### Cited Findings
- Anti-Cyber Crime Department warning of 20 Jan 2025, quoted in VnExpress: "I won a reward just for answering some questions! You should do it now before the prize presentation ceremony ends!" — [VnExpress, 2025-02-23](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/news/crime/cambodia-arrests-2-for-using-hun-sen-s-name-in-180-000-telegram-scam-4853163.html)
- Fake Hun Sen Telegram channel (arrests on 21 Feb 2025):
  - A fake channel mimicking Hun Sen's official channel solicited "investments" with high short-term returns.
  - About $180,000 was lost.
  - Source: [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/news/crime/cambodia-arrests-2-for-using-hun-sen-s-name-in-180-000-telegram-scam-4853163.html)
- Fake French aid scam on Telegram, with a phishing link:
  - Khmer text: «បារាំងផ្ដល់ជំនួយ ៣០០លានអឺរ៉ូលើវិស័យអាទិភាពមួយចំនួននៅកម្ពុជា អំណោយដល់ប្រជាពលរដ្ឋកម្ពុជា $1.500»
  - Fact Crescendo's English rendering: "France provides 300 million euros in aid in some priority areas in Cambodia; donations to the people of Cambodia total 1,500 USD each".
  - Sources: [Fact Crescendo KM, 2024-01-23](https://cambodia.factcrescendo.com/a-scam-of-french-donation-spreading-on-telegram/); [EN](https://cambodia.factcrescendo.com/english/a-scam-of-french-donation-spreading-on-telegram/)
- Fake Marshall promotion posted by a Cambodian Facebook page on 2024-10-07: "SORRY! Apologies to customers for Marshall's birthday promotion leading to exhaustion due to many people queuing. We would like to learn from experience and open an online promotion and only the first 200 customers will receive an 80% discount on Marshall MIDDLETON speakers." — [Fact Crescendo](https://cambodia.factcrescendo.com/english/consumer-safety-fact-check/marshall-products-exploited-in-social-media-phishing-scams-619481/)
- Coca-Cola survey scam (June 2021, older): a link titled «មូលនិធិសុខុមាលភាពកូកា-កូឡា» ("Coca-Cola welfare fund") offered a prize for a four-question survey. Cambodia Beverage Company denied involvement on 3 Jun 2021. — [Fact Crescendo KM](https://cambodia.factcrescendo.com/did-coca-cola-in-cambodia-provide-prize-for-an-online-survey/)
- Moses Ngeth's dataset of scam-compound job ads:
  - 4,991 ads from 37 Telegram channels, April 2023 to July 2026.
  - Coded job roles: "add contact", "model real face", "model AI", "killer", "money mule", "AI model developer".
  - Pay ran from $600/month for "add contact" to $7,000/month for a female model with a "Western face".
  - Quoted ad: "Your passport and cellphone will NOT be held … You're free to go WHENEVER you want".
  - Sources: [ABC, 2026-09-25](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-25/cambodia-scam-industry-job-advertisements/107185964); [CamboJA](https://cambojanews.com/cambodia-scam-recruitment-continued-despite-closure-claim-researcher-says/)
- CamboJA's own investigation (Feb 2026):
  - A Telegram group of more than 17,000 members posted about 4,100 job ads in one week.
  - Vague roles included "Add Contact", "Typing or Chatting", "AI or Real Model" and "Call Center".
  - Some ads said passports would be kept "for 'safekeeping'" and employees could not leave.
  - Source: [CamboJA](https://cambojanews.com/scam-recruitment-surges-in-cambodia-despite-crackdown-on-networks/)
- Khmer Times, from a search summary only (not read directly):
  - Fake loans "of up to $2,500 with promises of fast approval, simple requirements, no guarantor and low interest rates".
  - Victims are asked for an upfront fee "such as $500, purportedly to check their credit history".
  - Fake bank staff tell victims their account "has become 'damaged' or has a problem".
  - Source: [Khmer Times 501936986](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/501936986/police-issue-warning-over-fake-loans-and-bank-officers/)
- Khmer Times, from a search summary only, on a "fake debt" shaming scam:
  - The scammer asks whether the victim knows someone with a generic Khmer name ("Lekhena or Dara").
  - The scammer then claims that person named the victim as a loan "guarantor or co-debtor".
  - The victim is threatened with a shaming Facebook post that includes their photo.
  - Source: [Khmer Times 501456319](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/501456319/scammers-using-public-shaming-threat-to-blackmail-victims/)
- EDC imposters, from EDC's notice of 11 Sep 2023: callers contacted customers by phone or social media and asked for payment "through Wing or True Money for monthly bills or to change electricity meters or circuit breakers", sometimes "to avoid temporary power outages". — [Phnom Penh Post, 2023-09-13](https://www.phnompenhpost.com/national/edc-release-warnings-falsified-bills)
- Tax imposters: GDT warned about people posing as tax officials and told businesses to check documents with GDT "Check & Track" QR scanning (search summary only). — [Khmer Times 501547045](https://khmertimeskh.com/501547045/gdt-warns-the-public-about-tax-fraud-scam)

### Inferences
- Most verbatim texts available in English are prize or aid lures and Telegram-takeover lures. Loan, fake-bank-staff and debt-shaming scripts appear only as paraphrase.
- Khmer-language lure texts are scarce online because warnings usually show screenshots. Fact Crescendo's Khmer pages are the best public source of transcribed Khmer lure text.

### Gaps
- I could not read full Khmer Times articles, so any exact Khmer Times quotes are missing.
- I found no UNODC, IJM or Global Anti-Scam Org report with Cambodian-language victim-facing message samples. Their reports focus on compounds and trafficking.
- Moses Ngeth's full report was not located online. Only the ABC and CamboJA coverage was read.

## Q4. What scam types are new or rising in Cambodia in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The clearest new domestic trends are:
- Malware delivered as Telegram files, with Khmer-named files (July 2026).
- Malicious APKs pushed through "watch videos, get paid" lures (Aug 2026).
- Extortion of Facebook Live shoppers by fake shop owners and fake police on Telegram (June 2026).
- Illegal scratch-card lottery livestreams (2026).
- Impersonation of senior officials for investment and prize lures (2025).
- Fake border-troop donation appeals (June 2025).

Deepfake and "AI model" video calls are well documented, but mostly aimed at foreign victims. I found no Cambodia-specific evidence for fake KHQR payment screenshots, SIM swap in 2025–26, sextortion or recovery scams. Details are in the Emerging scam types section below.

### Cited Findings
- On 11 Jul 2026 MPTC Minister Chea Vandeth warned against opening files with extensions .exe, .bat, .vbs, .ps1, .sh, .msi and .scr. The expert Chy Sophat said "scammers appear to be targeting Cambodian users by naming files and writing messages in Khmer". — [Cambodianess, 2026-07-14](https://cambodianess.com/article/one-click-total-control-cambodia-warns-of-telegram-malware-surge)
- Facebook Live shopper extortion:
  - The Anti-Cyber Crime Department arrested a suspect on 20 June 2026.
  - He watched live sales, made fake Telegram accounts posing as the shop owners, and told buyers they had "transferred money incorrectly and caused problems with the store's banking system", so the store's account was "temporarily blocked".
  - He also used fake accounts with photos of senior leaders and police to threaten victims.
  - He took more than $100,000 over about 50 incidents.
  - Sources (search summaries): [The Star/PPP, 2026-06-22](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/06/22/beware-facebook-shoppers-cambodian-police-bust-telegram-scam-over-us100000-extorted); [PPP](https://phnompenhpost.com/national/beware-facebook-shoppers-police-bust-telegram-scam-over-100k-extorted/)
- Two men were arrested for impersonating seven Phnom Penh police officials through fake Telegram accounts. — [Khmer Times 502023805](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/502023805/two-arrested-for-impersonating-phnom-penh-police-chiefs-to-scam-victims-on-telegram)
- Scratch-card lottery livestreams (search summary):
  - Operators used "accomplices posing as winners and fake money transfers during Facebook livestreams".
  - In a June operation in Banteay Meanchey, police found at least eight Facebook pages.
  - Source: [PPP](https://phnompenhpost.com/national/no-chicken-dinner-police-expose-lottery-jackpot-social-media-fraudsters/)
- On 2026-03-24 the Anti-Cyber Crime Department named Facebook pages behind online scratch lotteries, including “ឆ្នោតកោសចែកលុយពិតៗ002”, “LuckyDay 2026 – Hari Keberuntunganmu”, “New Year 168” and “កោសំណាង – LuckyScratch88”. — [HNA News, 2026-03-24](https://www.hotnews-asia.com/detail/640841)
- Fake border-troop donations: a man was held after he "solicited money under the guise of raising funds to support frontline border troops" on Facebook. He was arrested on 27 June 2025 (search summary). — [Phnom Penh Post](https://phnompenhpost.com/national/man-in-custody-after-soliciting-fake-donations-for-border-troops-sharing-false-information/)
- Android banking trojan run as a service from the K99 Triumph City compound in Sihanoukville (Infoblox, 2026-04-10):
  - It impersonates banks and government agencies.
  - It intercepts SMS OTPs and captures facial-recognition data during fake KYC checks.
  - Its targets are mainly outside Cambodia (Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam and others). Infoblox gives no evidence that Cambodians are targeted.
  - Sources: [Infoblox](https://www.infoblox.com/blog/threat-intelligence/scams-slaves-and-malware-as-a-service-tracking-a-trojan-to-cambodias-scam-centers/); [Hackread](https://hackread.com/android-banking-trojan-cambodia-scam-compounds/)
- Deepfake and "model AI" video calls:
  - Scam-compound job ads recruit "model AI" workers who use AI face masks, and "AI model developer" roles. — [ABC, 2026-09-25](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-25/cambodia-scam-industry-job-advertisements/107185964)
  - Fake police-station sets, including a fake AFP office, were found at O'Smach for video-call authority scams. — [ABC, 2026-02-04](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-02-04/fake-afp-office-scam-centre-cambodia/106303328); [Bloomberg, 2026-03-12](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-12/cambodian-scammers-use-fake-police-stations-to-fool-victims)
- Scale of the crackdown: 663 scam sites closed, including 86 large compounds, and $300M frozen. Officials said criminals now work "from rented homes, rented rooms, hotels, even in cars". — [ABC, 2026-09-25](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-25/cambodia-scam-industry-job-advertisements/107185964)
- The Law on Anti-Technology Fraud took effect on 7 Apr 2026. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/3/cambodia-parliament-approves-law-to-combat-cybercrime-scam-rings)

### Inferences
- Crackdowns have pushed operations into smaller, dispersed settings, so more domestically aimed Khmer-language scams are plausible. This is inferred from the ABC quote and has not been measured.
- Telegram account takeover is the gateway to several downstream scams: family impersonation, fake loans and investment spam from compromised contacts. A benchmark should include takeover lures such as the fake Telegram verification message, the Premium gift and malicious files.

### Gaps
- I found no Cambodian source on fake KHQR payment-success screenshots or edited receipts. Only ABA's QR-sticker overlay notice exists.
- I found no Cambodia-specific sextortion warning (search results only mentioned impersonation-based extortion). I also found none on recovery scams. The Khmer Times piece "Scammers use our scam awareness against us" (501522578) may cover recovery or fake-helper scams but could not be read.
- I found nothing on fake NSSF or pension scams, fake government subsidy SMS (other than the French aid hoax), or fake rental or booking scams in 2025–26.
