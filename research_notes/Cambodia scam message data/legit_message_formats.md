# Legitimate Cambodian message formats that a rule-based scam detector could flag (false-positive test set)

Research date: 2026-09-28. Scope: Cambodia, 2023–2026, Khmer and English. Personal names, account numbers and phone numbers are redacted. Search results were thin for exact SMS wording. Most bank, telco and SMS-archive sites block scripted fetches (ababank.com, trc.gov.kh and sms24.me return 403 or a Cloudflare challenge). The strongest verbatim material came from three places: (a) bank announcement pages that were reachable (BIDC, Vattanac, KB PRASAC, LOLC, MB Cambodia, ACLEDA); (b) open-source GitHub projects that parse **real ABA Telegram payment alerts**; (c) Cambodia Expats Online forum threads.

## 1. OTP / verification-code wording (banks, wallets, telcos, Telegram, Grab, Facebook)

### Takeaway
I found verbatim OTP wording only for global apps (Telegram, Grab). For Cambodian banks, wallets and telcos I found only confirmation that codes are sent by SMS or push, and who sends them. Legitimate OTP messages are full of "code", "do not share", "log in" and "expires". Telegram in Cambodia also charges users a small fee to receive the login SMS, which is a real case of "pay a fee to get your code".

### Cited Findings
- Telegram's login-code message reads: "Do not give login code to anyone, even if they say they are from Telegram! This code can be used to log in to your Telegram account. We never ask it for anything else. If you didn't request this code by trying to log in on another device, simply ignore this message." — [Threads repost](https://www.threads.com/@nd_uk/post/C0eFXUwRxph?hl=en)
- Grab's SMS OTP reads: "521389 is your GRAB verification code. It expires in 2 minutes. Do not share it with anyone, including GRAB." This came from a search-result snippet of a public SMS-receive archive. The page itself was Cloudflare-blocked, so I could not re-check it. — [sms24.me Grab](https://sms24.me/en/messages/Grab/)
- Cambodian users report that Telegram would not send a login SMS to a Cambodian number unless they bought Telegram Premium ($1 for one week; another user saw $1.50). The stated reason was the high cost of SMS to Cambodia. One user received the PIN by an automated call from Turkey after paying. — [Cambodia Expats Online](https://cambodiaexpatsonline.com/ask-the-expats-questions-answers/telegram-sms-extortion-fee-any-workarounds-t61865.html)
- ABA says it sends "security codes" by SMS or push notification. It tells customers never to give them to anyone, "including those pretending to be ABA employees". It also says ABA will never send an email, SMS or link asking for card or i-Banking details. This is from ABA's news page as summarised by search; the page returned 403 to direct fetch. — [ABA Bank](https://www.ababank.com/en/aba-news/stay-protected-from-scammers-8/)
- ACLEDA's only legitimate SMS sender name is "ACLEDA". ACLEDA gives these as scam markers: SMS from an unknown NXSMS sender or phone number, the phrases "to verify again to protect your rights" or "please re-verify the account with 24 hours", and links to any domain other than acledabank.com.kh. — [ACLEDA security tips](https://www.acledabank.com.kh/kh/eng/security_tip_phishing)
- CamCERT (the MPTC unit) has a Khmer guide on what to do when you receive an OTP (MFA) code you did not request. Only the Khmer title was retrievable. — [CamCERT](https://www.camcert.gov.kh/en/otp/)
- Facebook's SMS code format ("[code] is your Facebook confirmation code") is widely described in consumer guides. I could not verify the current exact wording or whether a Khmer version exists. — [Zeru](https://zeru.com/blog/why-am-i-getting-a-text-with-a-facebook-confirmation-code)

### Inferences
- A rule that fires on "code" + "Telegram/ABA/Grab" will hit every genuine OTP. The detector should flag *requests to send/forward* a code ("send me the code", "ផ្ញើលេខកូដមកខ្ញុំ"), not messages that *contain* one alongside "do not share".
- The Telegram Premium fee is real and paid inside the app to Telegram, never by transfer to a person. A rule on "OTP + pay fee" should require that payment go to a person or account.

### Gaps
- I found no verbatim OTP SMS, in Khmer or English, for ABA, ACLEDA, Wing, Canadia, Prince, Bakong, TrueMoney, Pi Pay, Smart, Cellcard, Metfone, PassApp or Tada. Bank sites block fetches, public SMS archives are Cloudflare-protected, and Reddit blocks unauthenticated API access. The best way to fill this is to collect the team's own phones' inbox screenshots.

## 2. Bank transaction alerts, card alerts, MFI reminders, maintenance notices

### Takeaway
ABA's merchant payment alert has a stable, verifiable English format ("$X paid by NAME (*123) on DATE via ABA PAY/ABA KHQR at MERCHANT. Trx. ID: …, APV: …"). Bank maintenance notices use "temporarily suspended" language in both Khmer and English, and have no links. Both are strong false-positive tests.

### Cited Findings
- Formats that one repo labels "Real ABA notification formats": `$0.01 paid by … (*774) on Aug 22, 03:31 PM via ABA KHQR (BKRTKHPPXXX) at …. Trx. ID: 1787389102` and `៛12,000 paid by … (*493) on Aug 22, 02:39 PM via ABA PAY at …. Trx. ID: 17873843` — [GitHub HoutWill/khqr-payment-with-telegram-group](https://github.com/HoutWill/khqr-payment-with-telegram-group)
- The "real ABA notification shape" including Remark and APV: `$2.70 paid by CUSTOMER (*098) on Sep 05, 04:09 PM via ABA PAY at MERCHANT. Remark:  . Trx. ID: 178859934628331, APV: 964372.` The same repo's parser also matches the Khmer label `លេខប្រតិបត្តិការ:` (transaction number), which suggests a Khmer-language variant exists. — [GitHub Terminators-S/khqr-Tele-webhook](https://github.com/Terminators-S/khqr-Tele-webhook)
- A shorter variant: `$5.00 paid by … (*476) on Jun 23 via ABA PAY. Trx. ID: 178220228091798, APV: 400834.` — [GitHub seavfoueang-coc/abapay_integration](https://github.com/seavfoueang-coc/abapay_integration)
- ABA PayWay merchants can get notifications on Telegram, in the ABA Merchant app, on a POS terminal, in the ABA Mobile app or by email. — [PayWay](https://www.payway.com.kh/)
- ACLEDA sends an SMS after every account transaction: deposit or withdrawal, local or overseas transfer, cheque, standing order, overdraft, and counter, ATM or POS payment. The SMS is **in English only, max 160 characters**. — [ACLEDA Message Alert](https://www.acledabank.com.kh/kh/eng/ps_ebsmsalert)
- ACLEDA Super App can read aloud incoming and outgoing amounts in Khmer, English or Chinese. — [ACLEDA sound alert](https://www.acledabank.com.kh/qr/toanchet-manual/sound-alert?l=eng&h=0)
- BIDC maintenance notice (29 Jul 2026), in English and Khmer: "local transfer via Bakong and KHQR services will be temporarily suspended 3 hours from 22h00 on 30 July 2026 to 01h00 on 31 July 2026 for system maintenance". The Khmer version uses "ផ្អាកដំណើរការជាបណ្តោះអាសន្ន" (temporarily suspended) and contains spelling slips ("តំហែទាំ", "អាស្រយ័"). — [BIDC EN](https://www.bidc.com.kh/en/article/bakong-khqr-system-maintenance-3-hours-from-22h00-on-30-07-2026-to-01h00-on-31-07-2026); [BIDC KM](https://www.bidc.com.kh/km/article/bakong-khqr-system-maintenance-3-hours-from-22h00-on-30-07-2026-to-01h00-on-31-07-2026)
- A forum thread titled "NBC Bakong maintenance 11pm-1am 26-Mar-2026" exists. ABA users report that app and ATM maintenance "happens every month", often around payday. One user got an ABA app message that "a card transaction was declined because of a lack of funds". — [Cambodia Expats Online](https://cambodiaexpatsonline.com/general-chatter/aba-bank-maintenance-t40901.html)

### Inferences
- Bank alerts carry 6–15 digit IDs (Trx. ID, APV) next to money words. Digit-run rules meant to catch OTPs will false-positive unless they need a *request* verb.
- Real Khmer bank notices have typos. Do not use Khmer spelling errors as a scam signal.
- A genuine maintenance or suspension notice has no link, no "verify" and no deadline for the customer. Requiring those extra signals keeps the suspension rule precise.

### Gaps
- I found no verbatim Wing, Canadia, Prince, TrueMoney, Pi Pay or Bakong-app "you received" alert text, and no Khmer ABA alert text.
- I found no public example of an SMS repayment reminder from PRASAC, AMK, Hattha or LOLC. The JSONL entry for this is an unsourced template.

## 3. Telco promotional SMS, top-ups, SIM registration

### Takeaway
Telcos run real "free" and "win" campaigns and send real top-up and expiry SMS. The regulator also ordered telcos to contact, and eventually deactivate, subscribers with unregistered SIMs. So "your number will be suspended unless you register" can be legitimate. I found no verbatim SMS text.

### Cited Findings
- Smart's promotions page (accessed 2026-09-28) lists: "Tov Pchum with Smart 5G 2026 — Play twice for free every day for a chance to win a Vespa, smartphones, and more"; "Log in to SmartNas every day for your chance to win 2 round-trip AirAsia tickets … or an OPPO A6X 5G phone in our monthly draws!"; "Get 1GB Data in SmartNas for FREE Every Day"; and "Get a Special ABA Account Number FREE". These were quoted through a fetch summariser. — [Smart promotions](https://www.smart.com.kh/promotions)
- Smart gives bonus data and on-net minutes with every top-up on some plans (search summary of Smart's plans page). — [Smart plans](https://www.smart.com.kh/plans)
- On 30 Jun 2025 the TRC reported user complaints about operator SMS: SMS asking users to top up while their service was still valid, delayed service SMS, and confirmation SMS for services never subscribed to. TRC also required all operator SMS notifications to be in Khmer and/or the language chosen at SIM registration (search summary; page Cloudflare-blocked). — [TRC announcement](https://www.trc.gov.kh/en/media/news-releases/4-news-release/)
- The TRC ordered telcos to contact all subscribers with unregistered SIMs, or with several SIMs under one ID, to collect ID documents, and to deactivate numbers that lack proper documentation. — [Developing Telecoms](https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/16689-cambodian-regulator-cracks-down-on-unregistered-sim-cards.html)
- Metfone has a "verify your SIM" page (/page/verifyyoursim, Khmer title "លេខទូរសព្ទរបស់អ្នក អត្តសញ្ញាណឌីជីថលរបស់អ្នក"). Its content is rendered by JavaScript and could not be extracted. — [Metfone](https://metfone.com.kh/en/page/verifyyoursim)

### Inferences
- Telco promo SMS in Khmer will likely contain "ឥតគិតថ្លៃ" (free), "ទទួលបាន" (receive) and "ឈ្នះ" (win), because TRC now requires Khmer. The "you won" rule should need an extra signal, such as a fee, a link to a non-official domain or a request for personal data.
- SIM-registration reminders legitimately combine suspension, a deadline and "verify". What separates them from scams is that the action is a store visit or an official app, not a link plus payment.

### Gaps
- I found no verbatim Smart, Cellcard or Metfone SMS (top-up confirmation, bonus, expiry, SIM registration) in either language. These need collecting from real handsets.

## 4. Delivery / logistics / e-commerce notifications

### Takeaway
I found no verbatim Cambodian courier SMS. J&T Cambodia and Cambodia Post do send delivery-status notifications, and cash-on-delivery amounts are normal. The JSONL has templates only.

### Cited Findings
- J&T Express Cambodia runs an app with SMS and push delivery alerts (search summary of the store listing). — [J&T Cambodia App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kh/app/j-t-cambodia/id6446994804)
- Cambodia Post items show an "Awaiting Pickup" status at a Cambodia Post facility. — [Parcelsapp Cambodia Post](https://parcelsapp.com/en/carriers/cambodia-post)

### Inferences
- Legitimate COD messages mention an amount owed to the courier on delivery. The "parcel + upfront fee" rule should look for payment *before* delivery, through a link or an account transfer.

### Gaps
- I found no wording for Virak Buntham, Grab Express, foodpanda, Nham24, ZTO, Shopee or Khmer24.

## 5. Government notices (traffic fines, tax, MPTC, NSSF, health, elections)

### Takeaway
Traffic fines can now be paid through the Canadia Bank app or at Canadia branches, within 15 days. So a genuine police + fine + pay + deadline notice exists. I found no verbatim SMS or Telegram text for any government sender.

### Cited Findings
- National Police and Canadia Bank signed an MoU. Citizens can pay traffic fines through a mobile application or at Canadia branches nationwide. Some citizens previously "did not know where to pay after receiving notices". — [Khmer Times](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/502018036/cambodia-tightens-traffic-law-enforcement-expands-fine-payment-options)
- The fine-payment window was cut from 30 to 15 days (search summary of Khmer Times coverage). — [Khmer Times](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/502018036/cambodia-tightens-traffic-law-enforcement-expands-fine-payment-options)
- The National Road Safety Committee runs the Telegram group @traffic6789 ("Our Road Safety") for reports and legal matters. — [Khmer Times](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/501003318/users-warned-not-to-post-false-info-in-traffic-telegram-group/)
- GDT runs e-Filing, a taxpayer app and GDT e-Administration. I found no evidence of GDT SMS reminders. — [GDT](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en)
- MPTC publicly warns about malicious Telegram links, files and account theft. Forwarded copies of these warnings are themselves keyword-dense. — [Khmer Times](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/501979323/cambodia-warns-of-malicious-telegram-links-files-and-account-theft/)

### Inferences
- The police-fine rule should exempt messages that point to Canadia Bank or official channels, and flag payment to personal accounts or wallets or to unknown links.

### Gaps
- I found no verbatim traffic-fine notice (SMS, Telegram or paper) and nothing for GDT, NSSF, the National Election Committee or the Ministry of Health. I found no evidence that Cambodia sends nationwide government broadcast SMS.

## 6. Real promotions / lucky draws by banks and brands (2023–2026)

### Takeaway
Cambodian banks and MFIs routinely publish "Win! Win! Win!", "Congratulations … winners", "double their money weekly" and "You win a Toyota" wording, sometimes with bit.ly links. This is the richest verbatim source for testing "prize" and "guaranteed profit" false positives, in both English and Khmer.

### Cited Findings
- Vattanac Bank KHQR merchant campaign (Oct 2023): "Congratulations! We are delighted to announce our lucky draw winners for week 7." Existing merchants register "by clicking this link: https://bit.ly/Register-KHQR". The T&Cs say merchants "stand a chance to double their money weekly" (100% cashback, max USD 100). — [Vattanac Bank](https://www.vattanacbank.com/merchants/week-7-lucky-draw-winners-for-vattanac-bank-khqr-merchant-campaign)
- KB PRASAC: "Win! Win! Win! Winning chance for existing and new KB PRASAC merchants…" The Khmer version begins "ឈ្នះ! ឈ្នះ! ឈ្នះ!". Draws are held in the first week of each month. — [KB PRASAC EN](https://www.kbprasacbank.com.kh/en/media/promotion/lucky-draw-for-kb-prasac-merchants/); [KB PRASAC KM](https://www.kbprasacbank.com.kh/km/media/promotion/lucky-draw-for-kb-prasac-merchants/)
- LOLC, 1 Apr–30 Jun 2025: "Get paid faster with the chance to win motorbikes from LOLC!" The campaign gave three Honda Dream motorcycles, and LOLC KHQR registration is "free of charge". — [LOLC](https://www.lolc.com.kh/en/details/lolc-khqr-promotion)
- MB Cambodia: "Stand a chance to win many BIG prizes … or refer your family and friends". The draw is monthly and winners are announced on MB Cambodia's official Facebook page. — [MB Cambodia](https://www.mbcambodia.com/en/promotion/chance-to-win-big-prizes-with-mbcambodia-my-bank-app)
- BIDC 2025: "The top 10 KHQR merchants … will receive amazing prizes!" The Khmer title is "ឱកាសឈ្នះវង្វាន់ទ្វេរដង…" and the prizes are in riel (1st prize 1,000,000 riel). BIDC also ran the Nov 2023 Facebook minigame "You win a Toyota Raize – I win 150$", with "lucky code DG0xxxx". — [BIDC 2025 EN](https://www.bidc.com.kh/en/article/special-promotions-for-bidc-khqr-merchants-in-2025); [BIDC 2025 KM](https://www.bidc.com.kh/km/article/special-promotions-for-bidc-khqr-merchants-in-2025); [BIDC minigame](https://www.bidc.com.kh/en/article/minigame-you-win-a-toyota-raize-i-win-150)
- Wing Bank ran a WingPay/Wing KHQR draw from 15 Mar to 15 Sep 2024. It had weekly winners, a grand prize of a Ford Ranger XLT 2023, and total prizes up to 2,200 million riel (search summary; page now 404). — [Wing Bank](https://www.wingbank.com.kh/en/pay-with-wingpay-or-wing-khqr-and-win-exciting-prizes/)
- Hattha Bank ran a lucky draw for KHQR merchants and scanning customers. The page was not retrievable. — [Hattha Bank](https://www.hatthabank.com/media/promotion/13)

### Inferences
- Legitimate campaigns never ask winners to pay a fee to claim. Vattanac says "The fund will be automatically credited to the winners' accounts". The "you won" rule should key on *claim fee / tax / deposit to receive prize*, not on "win/congratulations".
- "double their money" appears in a real bank's T&Cs. Guaranteed-profit rules should target investment framing ("invest", "daily return %", "guaranteed").

### Gaps
- The Wing and Hattha pages were not retrievable. I found no SMS versions of any bank campaign announcements; all the wording above is from web and Facebook-style posts.
