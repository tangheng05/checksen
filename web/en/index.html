Common scam signs in Cambodia

Banks, ministries and police in Cambodia warn about these tricks again and again.

  • Asks for an OTP, PIN or password, even when it claims to be your bank.

  • Asks for a fee, tax or deposit before you get a loan, job or prize.

  • Threatens to freeze your account, or cut your power or water, unless you act now.

  • Sends an app (.apk) on Telegram and says it's from your bank.

  • Claims to be a relative with a new number, or says a relative had an accident, and asks for money.

  • Offers high-paid typing, chat or call-centre work in Poipet, Sihanoukville, Bavet or Thailand.

  • Asks strangers to donate to a personal account for someone who is sick.

  • Asks you to send money before a call or a meeting.

Questions people ask

How can I tell if a KHQR code is real or edited?

Send a photo of the QR to CheckSen. It shows the real recipient name, account, bank and amount, and checks the code's check digits. Before paying, always check the recipient's name in your own bank app.

Do banks ever ask for my OTP code by phone or Telegram?

No. Real banks and companies never ask for your OTP code, PIN or password. Anyone who asks for them is most likely a scammer.

Is CheckSen free? Do I need to install an app?

It's free and there's nothing to install. Open @CheckSen_bot in Telegram and send it a message, link, photo or file.

I already paid a scammer. What should I do?

Call your bank right away on the number on your card or in its app, and ask them to stop the transfer. Keep screenshots of the messages and the payment receipt, and report it to the police. Don't pay anyone who promises to get your money back for a fee.

Can CheckSen tell me a message is safe?

No. It can only say whether it found known scam signs. A new trick may not be caught yet, so when in doubt, check through your bank's official number or app.

Can CheckSen check a file or app for viruses?

Yes. Send the file or app to CheckSen. It looks inside to see what the file really is, so a “PDF” that is really a program gets caught, shows what an Android app is allowed to do, like reading your SMS, and checks it with ClamAV, VirusTotal and MalwareBazaar. A brand-new virus may not be known yet, so only open files from people you trust. Files over 100 MB are checked by name and type only.

What happens when I tap 🚩 Report as a scam?

CheckSen keeps only that phone number, Telegram username, website or QR account, with your Telegram ID scrambled (hashed). Your message is never kept. Once 3 different people report it, CheckSen warns anyone who sends it. Send /forget to delete your reports.

Can I add CheckSen to a family or work group?

Yes. Add @CheckSen_bot to the group. It stays quiet until someone mentions @CheckSen_bot, replies to one of its messages, or replies to a message with /check.

Your messages are not kept.

CheckSen checks messages and images in memory and doesn't save them. Its code is open, so anyone can check this on GitHub.
