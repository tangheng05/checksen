Nothing else is stored. The code is open on GitHub.

Messages and images
Not saved. The reply is kept in memory for an hour, keyed only by a hash of the content.
Links
Checked with Google Safe Browsing and abuse.ch's URLhaus, which say whether they are known phishing or malware sites.
Files
Checked in memory and deleted. ClamAV runs on our own server; VirusTotal and MalwareBazaar get only the file's fingerprint (a hash), never the file. Links inside are checked without being opened.
AI checks
Text and screenshots are sent to Google Gemini's free tier. Google may use them to improve its products and people may review them.Hide account numbers and personal details before you send.
Reports
Stored against that QR account, phone number, username or website, linked only to a keyed hash of your Telegram ID. Shown only after 3 different people report it, and our team reviews each one. Send /forget to delete yours.
